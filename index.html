Where does Lean help a local coding builder?

Two experiments tested Lean at different points in a local coding workflow. A formal brief and an optional helper yielded no measured gain in accepted patches. Requiring a proof before dispatch sharply reduced acceptance on the tested tasks.

What it costs to ask a small model whether search found the answer

When a Shared Memory search matches the wording of a question, the records may still not hold the answer. This note times GLiNER2.5-Decide making that check on real-length searches, measured 9 November 2026 on a desktop CPU and an Intel Arc Pro B70. It reports the cost, and how far the numbers can be trusted. It does not test whether the judgement is accurate.
